# 🛡️ DIGCON SEC · CyberSmart

**A beginner-friendly cybersecurity awareness game — think before you click.**

CyberSmart is a free, offline-capable, browser-based game that teaches everyday cyber safety through realistic scenarios. It's built for anyone — students, parents, small business owners, teachers, corporate employees, and general members of the public — regardless of technical background.

Play it locally, host it on GitHub Pages, or run it on a projector for a community training session. No installation. No accounts. No data collected.

---

## 🎮 What's inside — 6 games

| Game | What it teaches |
|---|---|
| 🕵️ **Phishing Detective** | Spot the phish in a Gmail/Outlook/Yahoo-style inbox. 5 escalating levels. |
| 🌍 **Everyday Cyber Safety** | Fake bank alerts, OTP theft, giveaway scams, delivery fraud, public Wi-Fi, password reuse. |
| 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Family Protector** | Fake emergency calls, WhatsApp impersonation, child online safety, elderly-targeted scams. |
| 🏪 **Business Defender** | Fake transfer receipts, supplier scams, ransomware, USB drops, customer data leaks. |
| 🔎 **Spot the Danger** | Identify the warning sign in suspicious ads, DMs, emails, and profiles. |
| 💡 **What Would You Do?** | Lost phone, ransomware, card fraud, account takeover, cyberbullying — pick the safest response. |

Each session is short (~10 questions or 5 phishing levels). Feedback appears immediately after every answer — **why** it's safe or unsafe, **what could happen** if you got it wrong, and **what to do** to protect yourself.

## 🎯 Purpose

Most cybersecurity training is boring, technical, or written for IT staff. It doesn't help the person who just got a "your account will be blocked" SMS, or the market trader who accepted a fake transfer screenshot.

CyberSmart fixes that by:

- **Simulating real situations** people actually encounter daily — not abstract definitions
- **Teaching through decisions**, not lectures
- **Speaking plain English** — no jargon required to play
- **Using familiar Nigerian and international examples** — UBA alerts, WhatsApp scams, POS fraud, "CEO gift-card" emails
- **Fitting in 15–30 minutes** — short enough to run in a classroom, lunch break, or community event

The goal is behaviour change, not a score. A player who finishes CyberSmart should walk away recognizing the four or five tactics that scammers use most — urgency, impersonation, lookalike links, and requests for money or codes — and know exactly what to do next time.

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Two game paths** — phishing inbox simulation + 5 scenario-based games
- ✅ **6 games, 5 categories, 50+ scenarios** (easily expandable — see below)
- ✅ **Randomised questions and answer order** every session — no repeats
- ✅ **Realistic inbox UI** — choose Gmail, Outlook, or Yahoo
- ✅ **Immediate educational feedback** — why, what to do, what could happen, "did you know?"
- ✅ **Score with penalties** — wrong answers cost 10% of the question's points
- ✅ **Rank and category breakdown** at the end of each session
- ✅ **Saved scores** — your best runs persist in the browser (localStorage)
- ✅ **Fully offline** — no internet needed once loaded
- ✅ **Zero dependencies** — one HTML file, no frameworks, no CDN, no tracking
- ✅ **Mobile-first** — works on phones, tablets, laptops, and projectors
- ✅ **No personal data collected** — no nickname, no email, no accounts
